Scrolling staff

One grand staff, the whole loop on a single line. The playhead never moves; the music slides under it.

1 · What you see

Green and red are your notes as you play them, amber is the note under the line, grey is what is coming. The click and the notes line up with the amber line exactly, because the strip moves the same number of pixels for every beat.
