Out toggle

Where the notes go: to the piano over MIDI, or out of the computer's own speakers. One control with one implementation (src/outtoggle.js), built into the empty <span id="outsel"> that every transport bar carries — the script overwrites that span's class with seg and writes <b>Out:</b> plus two buttons into it. Nothing about it is per-page.

In the bar — one height
30 px
Out:
The strip stands beside plain buttons and has to measure the same as them. Its halves are padding:6px 9px over a fixed line-height:16px — 28 px of button — and .seg's own 1 px border adds the last two: 28 + 2 = 30, the height of every button in the bar. The border belongs to the strip, not to the halves: the halves carry border:0 and a single 1 px --line seam between them, so the two faces touch and the corner radius is cut once, by the strip's overflow:hidden.
Phone — the same markup, twice the target
Out:
On the phone the identical span is restyled rather than rebuilt: 44 px tall (a finger, not a mouse), 8 px radius instead of 6, halves at 14 px with padding:0 14px and the strip's height doing the vertical centring, the Out: plate at 600 instead of 500. The lit half goes to 700 — the phone's one weight difference from the laptop. Same amber, same #14161a ink, same seam.

Rules. The Out strip is one control, not two buttons: one border, one radius, one seam — but it is not a fixed box. Its width follows its words, and because the lit half is set at 600 the strip breathes about two pixels as the choice moves (162.47 → 166.64) and twenty when Computer becomes Phone. Everything to its right in the bar shifts by that much, and the status line is what absorbs it. Lit is always amber #e8b44a with #14161a ink — 600 on the laptop, 700 on the phone — and unlit is always the ordinary button face #2a2f3a, so the strip reads at a glance from across the room. Both halves can be lit at once; the strip must never be drawn as if exactly one is. A half that cannot be chosen is repainted as well as faded: --dim on #23272f with a not-allowed cursor, the one disabled treatment in the app that is a colour swap and not only an opacity. The Out: label lives inside the strip on its own #20242c plate at 11 px/500 — never above it, never beside it as a separate span. The second half is named for whatever is actually going to make the sound (Computer, or Phone while one is mirroring); the first is always Piano. In a bar the strip measures 30 px like every button next to it, and on a phone 44 px like every other target.
