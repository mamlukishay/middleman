One block, not two buttons. The container owns the 2px corners and the single continuous 5px side; the halves own only their faces and the one 1px seam between them. No gap. Only one half is ever down, and the half that is down travels the whole 5px, covers the side in its own column and turns amber with a warm 1px inset line. A half you cannot press is down too, but on a face a shade darker than the panel: pushed in, dim, and not coming back. Both halves carry the same weight in both states — 600 whether they are up or down — so picking a face never re-measures the block and nothing beside it moves; the same is true of .modes2, where a two-word label wraps identically either way. The strip's name is out of flow above the block, in the line the bar's top padding reserves.
