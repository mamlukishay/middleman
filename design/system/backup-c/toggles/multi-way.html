Three or four, the block does not change: one continuous 5px side under the whole strip, 2px on the outer corners only, a 1px seam at each cut, and exactly one half down. The nav is made of links rather than buttons so it works before a module has loaded, and it takes the full 180px of the sidebar; the views strip is the small size — 11px on 3px / 10px of padding — because it sits in the ruler above the stage, not in the bar. A strip that is in the transport bar — the OUT block — is held to two more rules these two do not need: its name sits out of flow above the faces, and both halves are 600 in both states so the block cannot re-measure itself when you pick one. Here the weight is free to change, because the nav's halves are equal columns and the views strip has a row to itself.
