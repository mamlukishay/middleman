10 engraved — .k and .u: uppercase, .08em, 700, in #5d6473, the colour of a word cut into the panel rather than lit on it.
13 lit glass — the value: 700, .02em, the accent, with a 9 px amber glow behind it. Only numbers carry light.
The pocket itself sets 13 px and tabular figures for everything in it, so a reading never jitters as it counts.

Rules. 10 is always a label: uppercase, letter-spaced, 700, dim, and it sits above its control rather than beside it — inside a pocket the same 10 px is the engraved key, one step darker again at #5d6473. 13 is the control size and never changes with the button's size — only .mini drops to 11 — and it is also the pocket's size, so a value reads at the same weight as a button's word. Mono (--mono) is for a number the app is rewriting many times a second; everything else uses tabular figures in the sans face, which keeps the column without changing the voice.
