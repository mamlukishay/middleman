Spacing & rhythm

Five gaps, four radii, four depths, and one vertical rhythm that every laptop bar is built from.

Spacing

6siblings — between controls that belong to one group
10list rows — the sidebar's padding, a pocket's own side padding
14insets — the side padding of every bar, panel and stage
20groups — between whole groups of controls in a bar

Depth

--upstanding 5 px proud: a control you can press

--down-littravelled the whole 5 px: a toggle that is on

--down-offthe same flush geometry, a shade off the panel: dead

--pktthe opposite of --up: a hole milled into the panel, where a reading lives

The bar's rhythm
Out
speed100bpm
26 above = 8 + 10 + 3 + 5
14 below = 8 + 5 + 1
58 the transport square, and every toggle
29 the strip and the pockets
5 + 1 the side, and the base line it stands on the base line: everything in the bar stands on it

Rules. The bar is measured by the transport's square, --xport-h 58, and every toggle in it is that square. The split strip and the sunk pockets keep the face height, --btn-h 29, and the bar aligns to flex-end, so all of them stand on the one base line. Above the row the bar pads 8 + --cap 10 + --capgap 3 + --h 5 = 26: eight of air, then the line the strip's name hangs in, then the 5 px the block would need if it were the tallest thing. Below it pads 8 + --h 5 + 1 = 14: the side the block stands on, the one pixel of base it casts, and the same eight of air. The name is out of flow, so it can never push the block down.
