Rules. Stark, in three steps: face #2c313b on panel #1c1f26 on ground #14161a. Amber is the only "on" colour; red is only recording and only misses. The seven block colours are geometry, not palette — never paint anything else with them, and the same goes for the four pocket colours: #0b0e13 is a hole, #5d6473 is a word cut into the panel, #1b2027 is a notch with no light in it, and only a value gets the accent. The sheet is white, so the dark-chrome signals are re-tuned there: --hit washes out on white and becomes #16a34a, --rh becomes #b07d1a; --miss carries on both grounds and is left alone.
