What each part is
Nav
The three pages are one split block of links, full width, and the page you are on is the half that is down. It is <a>s, so it works before a module has loaded.
Song cards
6 px radius rows, 3 px apart: title on one line, the small line under it in dim. On is amber with the sub-line warmed to #5c4a1c; hover is a flat #242832 wash, no depth — a row is not a block.
Steps
10 px section labels in the foreground colour, then 11 px rows with a 7 px dot: grey while undone, green once done, and the current one carries the hover wash permanently.
The foot
Pushed down by margin-top:auto over a hairline: the Tutor / Free practice split block, the count, a 5 px progress bar, and one full-width mini button.

Rules: 10 px of padding, 16 px between the column's sections, and hair gaps inside one — 3 px between song cards, 2 px between steps. Only the blocks stand proud; cards, step rows and the progress bar are painted flat on the panel.
