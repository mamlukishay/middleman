Transport

One button does both jobs, and it is the biggest block in the bar: the same face, the same 5 px of side, the same flush amber when it is pushed in — only two faces tall and two of those wide. Its height is --xport-h (--btn-h × 2 = 58) and its width is twice that again, so Play and Stop are the same 116 × 58 whatever the word inside and nothing beside it can move.

Play — raised: grey face, 5 px of side, one hard base line on the panel. The word is inside the block, because this is the one control the eye should find without reading.
Stop — dropped the whole 5 px and flush with the panel, amber face, warm top line. The dashes are in the same two places: the block is the same 116 wide in both states, so the row behind it never moves.

Rules. The transport is 116 × 58 and carries its word; every other toggle in the bar is the 58 × 58 square, with a label that wraps inside it rather than stretching it. The strip and the pockets keep the face height, --btn-h 29, and stand on the same base line — the bar aligns to flex-end, so nothing has to be told where the bottom is. Vertical padding is zeroed everywhere and the label is centred by the box rather than by its own line box: left to line-height:normal a bold word, a musical sign and a pause bar each grew their own height.
