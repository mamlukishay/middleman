Record & outlined accent

Red is the one colour that is not amber, and it means exactly one thing: something is being written. The outlined family is the opposite — amber on the edge only, for the button that is merely the obvious next thing to do.

Rules. Red is only recording. It paints the whole face, takes white ink, and drops the full 5px with the plain dark inset line rather than the warm one, because the face underneath is not amber. Armed and rolling are one rule: the colour says "writing", the label says which part of the cycle you are in. The outlined family carries amber on the border and the text while the face stays grey — it reads as offered rather than engaged, and filling it in (.go) is the strongest thing the app ever says without pressing the block down.
