Surfaces

Everything in the app is a flat plane of one colour with a 1px --line border. There are seven planes between the page ground and a hovered control, each about one step lighter than the last, and nothing else: no shadow under a control, no gradient across a face, no lit edge, no depth. Hierarchy is the colour step plus the hairline.

The stack, ground to control
#14161aground — --bg, the page behind everything. Also the ink on an amber control.
#1c1f26panel — --pane: the sidebar, the bottom bar, the deck, the lesson card, a lane head, a strip cell.
#20242clabel plate — the Out: lozenge inside a strip. The one surface that exists to hold a word, not a control.
#22262finset — a control sunk below the face: .mini, .chip, kbd, .layer, a lesson slot, a selected lane head, a focused readout.
#23272fdead — a strip segment that cannot be pressed. One step off the inset, which is the whole signal.
#2a2f3acontrol face — every button, every .seg>a, the tooltip body. The plane a hand aims at.
#2c313bline — --line, the 1px border that separates every plane above from every plane below it. 54 uses.
#333947hover — the face, one step lighter. The only thing hover ever changes.
Two surfaces sit outside the ladder because they are rows, not planes: .trk:hover is #242832 (a sidebar row lighting up on the panel, not a face lighting up) and the tooltip's border is #3a4150 rather than --line, because it floats over the page rather than sitting in it.

One weight, one colour, one job. Every separator in the app is 1px solid var(--line). A structural edge and a control's own outline are drawn with the same stroke; nothing is 2px, nothing is dashed, and nothing uses a second line colour.

Not the language
no shadowa control is not above the panel, so it casts nothing on it
no gradienta face is one colour from edge to edge
no lit edge, no liftthe border is one colour; pressing changes colour, never position
The three shadows the app does own are not material. The tooltip has box-shadow:0 6px 18px rgba(0,0,0,.5) because it genuinely floats over the page; the MIDI LED and a key you are holding have a coloured glow, and #played a coloured text-shadow, because a glow means live right now. None of the three describes a surface, and no surface has one.

Rules. A surface is a flat fill from the stack — ground #14161a, panel #1c1f26, plate #20242c, inset #22262f, dead #23272f, face #2a2f3a, hover #333947 — and a 1px var(--line) border. Pick a step, do not invent one between two. Depth is read from the step alone: sunk means one step darker than the face, raised means one step lighter, and both keep the same border. Nesting stops at three: ground holds panels, a panel holds controls, and a control's inner planes stay inside its outline. Nothing gets a shadow, a gradient, a second border colour or a change of position — a glow is allowed only where it means live, never where it means shape.
